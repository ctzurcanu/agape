A community of creators, connected by what they know.

Agape organizes YouTube videos through an independent, growing map of topics. Creators place their own videos in topics, curate short TV programs of excerpts, write subtitle alternatives such as translations or commentary, discuss each other's work, and recommend the best contributions to their peers.

How creators join

  1. Sign in with Google. Agape asks for read-only access to YouTube so it can confirm which channel you own.
  2. Verify a video you own. Choose a public, embeddable video from your channel and the topic where it belongs. Agape checks with YouTube that the video belongs to your channel.
  3. Wait for review. An Agape administrator approves or declines the topic placement. You see the decision, its date and the reason in your creator studio.
  4. Take part. An approved placement lets you comment within that topic, add a moment of your video to the topic's TV, create named TV programs, and recommend other creators' work in topic ballots.

Why Agape asks for YouTube access

Agape uses the read-only YouTube scope only while you verify a video: it reads the list of channels you own and the details of the video you submitted (title, category, duration, privacy and embedding status). It does not upload, change or delete anything on YouTube, and it does not keep your Google access token. See the privacy policy for details.

Community rules

Creators can only recommend work they did not make. Recommendations stay private until a ballot closes. Moderation decisions and fines always come with a reason. The terms describe what is expected of everyone.
