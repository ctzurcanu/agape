Privacy policy

Effective 13 September 2026

This policy explains what Agape collects, why, who can see it, and how you can remove it. Agape is operated by Christian Tzurcanu (christian.tzurcanu@gmail.com).

Signing in

You sign in with your Google account through Supabase Auth. The sign-in service stores your email address, name, profile picture and Google account identifier. This sign-in service is shared with Allways, another application operated by the same operator; Agape's own data is kept separately and is not combined with Allways data.

YouTube data

When you verify a video, Agape uses Google's read-only YouTube permission (youtube.readonly) for that request only. It reads the channels owned by your Google account and the details of the video you submitted, to confirm that the video belongs to your channel and is public, embeddable and not a live broadcast. The Google access token is used during that request and is not stored. Agape has no background access to your YouTube account and never changes anything on YouTube.

Agape stores:

  • the channel's YouTube identifier and title;
  • the video's YouTube identifier, title, category and duration;
  • when the channel and video were verified;
  • the topics you proposed for the video and their review status.

Agape's use and transfer of information received from Google APIs adheres to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements. This information is used only to verify and display your contributions within Agape. It is not sold, not used for advertising, and not transferred to others except the service providers listed below. You can remove Agape's access at any time in your Google Account under Security → Your connections to third-party apps and services.

Your contributions

Agape stores what you create in it:

  • comments, and the topic in which you wrote them;
  • subtitle cues and their revision history, including who edited them;
  • topic suggestions and the decisions on them;
  • video moments, named TV programs, their published editions and subtitle versions;
  • ballot recommendations and their optional explanations;
  • fines and their reasons, and the contributions that make up your voting budget;
  • for administrators, the review decisions, ballots and fines they record, and a limit on how many videos can be verified per hour.

Who can see what

  • Approved videos, moments, comments, subtitles, topics, named TVs and their editions are public.
  • Pending or declined submissions are visible to you and to administrators. You see the reason for a decision, but not which administrator made it.
  • Ballot recommendations are sealed until the ballot closes. Afterwards, results show points and explanations without names; administrators can see who recommended what for auditing.

Storage in your browser

Agape keeps your sign-in session, your language choice and unsaved editing drafts in your browser's local storage. Agape does not use advertising or analytics cookies.

Service providers

  • Supabase hosts Agape's database, sign-in and verification function.
  • GitHub Pages serves the website.
  • YouTube plays embedded videos. Google may set cookies and collect data when you play them, under the Google Privacy Policy.

Deleting your data

In the creator studio, Delete my Agape data removes your verified channels and videos (with their topic placements, moments, creator subtitles and the comments others left on them), your comments, topic suggestions, named TVs and their editions, ballot recommendations, fines and administrator role. Work you shared with others stays without your name: subtitle lines on shared excerpts, subtitle versions on other people's TVs, editions you published for them, review decisions and ballots you created.

The button does not delete your sign-in account, because it is shared with Allways. To delete it as well, email christian.tzurcanu@gmail.com; requests are handled within 30 days. You can also ask for a copy of your data or a correction by email.

Retention

Agape keeps your data until you delete it or ask for its deletion. Public, anonymized records described above may remain.

Changes

If this policy changes, the new version will be published here with a new effective date.
